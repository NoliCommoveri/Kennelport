About AK Boston Terriers

Raised underfoot, loved for life

Our Bostons live in the house with us, not in a kennel building. Puppies are born in our living room, grow up with the sounds of a busy home, and meet kids, visitors and the vacuum before they ever meet you.

Health tested

Hearing, eyes, knees and the cataract gene, checked on every parent.

Home raised

Early handling, crate starts and potty-pad training before pickup.

Lifetime support

Questions at 2 a.m. or 12 years on, we answer, and we always take our dogs back.

About us

Our story

How a single Boston on the couch turned into AK Boston Terriers.

It started with Ziva. She was supposed to be our only dog, and within a year she'd convinced the whole family that a house isn't a home without a Boston in it.

We bred our first litter after two years of learning from mentors in the breed, joining our club, and health testing everything we could. Today we keep a small group of girls and one boy, plan one or two litters a year, and keep in touch with nearly every family who has taken one of our puppies home.

We breed for the Boston we love: a sturdy, square little dog with a kind eye, a clown's sense of humor and good manners in the house. Show quality matters to us, but temperament and health come first, every time.
